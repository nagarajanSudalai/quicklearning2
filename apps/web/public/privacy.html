Privacy

QuickStep is a learning app for children in Grades 1–5. We collect as little as possible, show no ads and use no third-party tracking or analytics.

Without an account

Everything a child does — stars, solved puzzles, streaks and test reports — is stored only on that device. Nothing is sent to us.

With a parent account

  • The parent's email address, used only to send a sign-in code. We never send marketing email.
  • Each child's first name or nickname and grade (up to 4 children). We do not ask for a child's age, photo, school or location.
  • Each child's learning progress: stars, which puzzles were solved, streaks and test scores, so it is the same on every device.

Only the signed-in parent can see their children's profiles and progress. Data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and is never sold or shared for advertising.

Deleting data

In the app, open For parents → Parent settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes the parent account, every child profile and all synced progress straight away. To clear a device, use Reset progress in the same screen, or remove the app.

Children

Children use QuickStep without giving any personal information. Accounts are created and managed by a parent, behind a check that young children cannot pass.

Contact

Questions about privacy: please contact the QuickStep team.
